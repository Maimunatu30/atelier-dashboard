Why Invert the Ratio in the Formula? When measuring customer leakage, a higher loss percentage represents higher friction. By formulating points as 33.3 × (Benchmark / Customers Lost), a brand operating exactly at benchmark achieves the full 33.3 points. If drop-off exceeds benchmark, points scale down proportionally, producing an intuitive 0–100 overall score (23.8 + 18.5 + 19.3 = 61.6 ≈ 62 / 100).

Question 01

How is the 62 / 100 Network Score calculated?

We evaluate 3 equal customer journey handoffs weighted at 33.3 points each: Search → Social (23.8 pts), Social → CRM (18.5 pts), and CRM → Commerce (19.3 pts). Points are derived using 33.3 × (Benchmark / Customers Lost), reflecting inverse friction against verified beauty retail benchmarks.

Question 02

Where does the $24 Million loss come from?

In the Social → CRM handoff, Atelier loses 27% of prospective customers versus the 15% industry benchmark—a 12% excess loss. Across a 1,000,000 audience pool entering social campaigns, this sheds 120,000 buyers. At a conservative $200 annual LTV, that directly creates a $24,000,000 annual revenue leakage.

Question 03

How does Network Score differ from Data Confidence?

Network Score (62/100) measures operational ecosystem health and latency across platforms. Data Confidence (95%) measures disclosure transparency and telemetry verification. Atelier's score is 95% verified because we audited their direct Meta, GA4, Shopify, and Klaviyo instances rather than relying on external guesswork.

Question 04

Can this model apply to enterprise brands like Sephora?

Yes, our demo dynamically evaluates 7 brands. For Sephora and Ulta, loyalty programs capture 80–95% of users, shifting their primary friction to CRM → Commerce (omnichannel store fulfillment and points redemption). For L'Oréal, multi-brand siloed databases create identity fragmentation across product lines.

Question 05

How does Digitas ensure zero double-counting in recovery?

Our financial waterfall uses sequential cohort mechanics: recovered visitors from Stage 1 must flow through baseline conversion before generating revenue; Stage 2 identity captures only expand direct-channel margin; and Stage 3 checkout recovery only applies to active cart sessions with completed suppression.

Question 06

What are Digitas's first 90-day technical deliverables?

Month 1: Server-Side Tagging (sGTM) and Meta CAPI to eliminate signal loss. Month 2: Frictionless 1-click social authentication and SMS capture to close the Social → CRM bottleneck. Month 3: Instant webhook push to Klaviyo replacing 30-min polling batches, unlocking $12M in Year 1 revenue.
