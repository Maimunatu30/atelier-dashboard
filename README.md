### 3. $24 Million Financial Loss Formula
Atelier's primary leakage occurs in the **Social → CRM** handoff, leaking **$24M annually**:

$$\mathbf{1,000,000\text{ customers}} \times (27\% - 15\%) \times \$200 = \mathbf{\$24,000,000 / \text{yr}}$$

- **Target Social Campaign Audience:** 1,000,000 prospective customers entering seasonal social campaigns.
- **Excess Customer Drop:** 27% lost vs. 15% benchmark = **12% excess loss** (120,000 lost direct customers).
- **Annual Customer Value (LTV):** \$200 average annual spend.
- **Identifiable Annual Leakage:** $120,000 \times \$200 = \mathbf{-\$24,000,000 / \text{yr}}$.
- **The Digitas Prize (50% Benchmark Closure Target):**
  - **+60,000 customers recovered**
  - **+$12,000,000 / yr Incremental Revenue**
  - **+$8,400,000 / yr Incremental Gross Profit** (at 70% gross margin)
  - **+$1,200,000 / yr Paid Ad Waste Recouped** (via real-time automated purchaser suppression)
  - **Total Net Annual Impact: $9,600,000 / yr**
